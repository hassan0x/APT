# ETW-TI Provider Investigation — WinDbg Cheatsheet

Locating the `Microsoft-Windows-Threat-Intelligence` provider in kernel memory and identifying its active consumers.

---

## Step 1 — Entry Point: `EtwpDebuggerData`

```text
dp nt!EtwpDebuggerData L4
```text

Reads 4 pointers from the ETW debugger data symbol. Take the **4th pointer** — that's the `_ETW_SILODRIVERSTATE` address, the root of all ETW state.

**Example output:**
```text
fffff803`4a2b0000  fffff803`4c8a1200
fffff803`4a2b0008  fffff803`4c8a1400
fffff803`4a2b0010  fffff803`4c8a1600
fffff803`4a2b0018  ffffb70a`79e3f000   ← _ETW_SILODRIVERSTATE (save this)
```text

---

## Step 2 — Dump ETW Silo State

```text
dt nt!_ETW_SILODRIVERSTATE ffffb70a`79e3f000
```text

`<4th pointer>` = `ffffb70a79e3f000` from Step 1. Formats the silo state structure. Note down two fields:
- `+0x1c8 EtwpLoggerContext` — pointer array of active ETW sessions → save this for Step 9
- `+0x1d0 EtwpGuidHashTable` — the value shown here is `EtwpGuidHashTable` itself → save as **HashTableBase** for Step 4

**Example output:**
```text
nt!_ETW_SILODRIVERSTATE
   +0x000 Cookie           : 0x45545753
   ...
   +0x1c8 EtwpLoggerContext : 0xffffb70a`7a120000   ← save for Step 9
   +0x1d0 EtwpGuidHashTable : [64] _ETW_HASH_BUCKET
                              @ ffffb70a`79e3f1d0   ← HashTableBase (save this)
```text

---

## Step 3 — Get Bucket Size

```text
?? sizeof(nt!_ETW_HASH_BUCKET)
```text

No address needed — pure type query. Expected: `0x38` (56 bytes).

**Example output:**
```text
unsigned int64 0x38
```text

---

## Step 4 — Jump to Bucket 29 (TI Provider's Bucket)

```text
dt nt!_ETW_HASH_BUCKET ffffb70a`79e3f1d0+0x658
```text

`<HashTableBase>` = `ffffb70a79e3f1d0` from Step 2. `+0x658` = `29 × 0x38` — the fixed offset for the TI provider's bucket. Shows the `ListHead` Flink — first `_ETW_GUID_ENTRY` in this bucket.

> Hash formula: `(Data1 ^ Data2 ^ Data4[0] ^ Data4[4]) & 0x3F = 0x1D`

**Example output:**
```text
nt!_ETW_HASH_BUCKET
   +0x000 ListHead :
      +0x000 Flink : 0xffffb70a`7b3c4200   ← first _ETW_GUID_ENTRY in bucket
      +0x008 Blink : 0xffffb70a`7b3c4200
   +0x010 Lock     : _EX_PUSH_LOCK
```text

---

## Step 5 — Search for TI Provider GUID in Memory

```text
s -b ffffb70a`79e3f1d0 L?0x2000000 7c 89 e1 f4 5d bb 68 56 f1 d8 04 0f 4d 8d d3 44
```text

`<StartAddress>` = use `HashTableBase` from Step 2 as a rough anchor, or any address in the kernel pool region. The search scans `0x2000000` bytes forward for the raw GUID bytes (little-endian). The hit address points to the `Guid` field — save it as **HitAddress** for Step 6. Only keep hits where `SiloState` matches the silo base from Step 1.

> TI Provider GUID: `{F4E1897C-BB5D-5668-F1D8-040F4D8DD344}`

**Example output:**
```text
ffffb70a`7b3c4228  7c 89 e1 f4 5d bb 68 56-f1 d8 04 0f 4d 8d d3 44   ← HitAddress (save this)
ffffb70a`7c1a0028  7c 89 e1 f4 5d bb 68 56-f1 d8 04 0f 4d 8d d3 44   ← ignore (wrong silo)
```text

> Two hits is normal — validate by checking `SiloState` in Step 6. Keep only the one matching `ffffb70a79e3f000`.

---

## Step 6 — Dump the GUID Entry

```text
dt nt!_ETW_GUID_ENTRY ffffb70a`7b3c4228-0x28
```text

`<HitAddress>` = `ffffb70a7b3c4228` from Step 5. The `Guid` field sits at `+0x28` inside the structure, so subtract `0x28` to get the **EntryBase** = `ffffb70a7b3c4200`. Confirm the `Guid` field matches the TI provider and `SiloState` at `+0x188` matches Step 1.

**Example output:**
```text
nt!_ETW_GUID_ENTRY @ ffffb70a`7b3c4200
   +0x000 List             : _LIST_ENTRY
   +0x010 RefCount         : 0n3
   +0x028 Guid             : {f4e1897c-bb5d-5668-f1d8-040f4d8dd344}   ← confirmed
   +0x060 ProviderEnableInfo : _TRACE_ENABLE_INFO
   +0x080 EnableInfo       : [8] _TRACE_ENABLE_INFO
   +0x188 SiloState        : 0xffffb70a`79e3f000   ← matches Step 1 ✓
```text

> **EntryBase = `ffffb70a7b3c4200`** — save this, used in Steps 7, 8, 10.

---

## Step 7 — Check Provider Enable State

```text
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x60
```text

`<EntryBase>` = `ffffb70a7b3c4200` from Step 6. `ProviderEnableInfo` sits at fixed offset `+0x60`. Key fields:
- `IsEnabled` — `1` = active, `0` = disabled
- `MatchAnyKeyword` — bitmask of subscribed TI events
- `Level` — verbosity level
- `LoggerId` — session consuming events

**Example output:**
```text
nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO @ ffffb70a`7b3c4260
   +0x000 IsEnabled        : 0x1       ← provider is active
   +0x004 Level            : 0x4 ''
   +0x008 MatchAnyKeyword  : 0xffffffffffffffff   ← all events subscribed
   +0x010 MatchAllKeyword  : 0
   +0x018 MatchAnyKeyword2 : 0
   +0x01c LoggerId         : 6         ← session ID (used in Step 9)
```text

---

## Step 8 — Walk All 8 Consumer Slots

```text
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x80
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0xa0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0xc0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0xe0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x100
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x120
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x140
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x160
```text

`<EntryBase>` = same as Step 7. `EnableInfo` array starts at `+0x80`, each slot is `0x20` bytes — 8 slots total. For each slot where `IsEnabled: 1`, note the `LoggerId` — you'll need it in Step 9.

**Example output:**
```text
# Slot 0 (+0x80) — ACTIVE
nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO
   +0x000 IsEnabled   : 0x1
   +0x01c LoggerId    : 0x6   ← Defender session

# Slot 1 (+0xa0) — ACTIVE
nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO
   +0x000 IsEnabled   : 0x1
   +0x01c LoggerId    : 0x7   ← another consumer

# Slot 2 (+0xc0) — inactive
nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO
   +0x000 IsEnabled   : 0x0   ← skip

# Slots 3–7 — all IsEnabled: 0x0, skip
```text

---

## Step 9 — Resolve Session from LoggerId

```text
dq ffffb70a`7a120000+(6*8) L1
dt nt!_WMI_LOGGER_CONTEXT ffffb70a`7a884000
```text

`<EtwpLoggerContext>` = `ffffb70a7a120000` from Step 2. `LoggerId` = `6` from Step 8. Multiply `6 × 8 = 0x30` to index into the pointer array, `dq` reads the session pointer, then dump it as `_WMI_LOGGER_CONTEXT`. Check `LoggerName` to identify the consumer.

**Example output:**
```text
# dq to get session pointer:
ffffb70a`7a120030  ffffb70a`7a884000   ← _WMI_LOGGER_CONTEXT pointer

# dt to dump the session:
nt!_WMI_LOGGER_CONTEXT @ ffffb70a`7a884000
   +0x000 LoggerName       : _UNICODE_STRING "DefenderApiLogger"   ← Defender confirmed
   +0x010 LoggerId         : 6
   +0x018 BufferSize       : 0x1000
   +0x020 NumberOfBuffers  : 4
```text

---

## Step 10 — Disable Provider / Consumer (Full KD Only)

**Disable all consumers (via ProviderEnableInfo):**
```text
eb ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x60 0
```text

**Disable a specific consumer slot:**
```text
eb ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x80 0    # Slot 0 (LoggerId 6 — Defender)
eb ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0xa0 0    # Slot 1 (LoggerId 7)
```text

`<EntryBase>` = `ffffb70a7b3c4200` from Step 6. Writes `0` to `IsEnabled`. Requires full kernel debugging over a named pipe — **LiveKD is read-only, this will not work there**.

**Example — verify after patch:**
```text
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO ffffb70a`7b3c4200+0x80
   +0x000 IsEnabled   : 0x0   ← patched, Defender blind to TI events
```text

## Quick Command Sequence

```text
dp nt!EtwpDebuggerData L4
dt nt!_ETW_SILODRIVERSTATE <SiloBase>
?? sizeof(nt!_ETW_HASH_BUCKET)
dt nt!_ETW_HASH_BUCKET <HashTableBase>+0x658
s -b <HashTableBase> L?0x2000000 7c 89 e1 f4 5d bb 68 56 f1 d8 04 0f 4d 8d d3 44
dt nt!_ETW_GUID_ENTRY <HitAddress>-0x28
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x60
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x80
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0xa0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0xc0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0xe0
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x100
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x120
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x140
dt nt!_TRACE_ENABLE_INFO <EntryBase>+0x160
dq <EtwpLoggerContext>+(LoggerId*8) L1
dt nt!_WMI_LOGGER_CONTEXT <Result>
```text
